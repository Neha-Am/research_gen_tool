## 🧾 IEEE Paper Format – Overview

IEEE format is a **structured academic writing style** used for engineering and technical research papers. It follows strict **layout**, **citation**, and **reference** formatting rules.

---

## 🗂️ 1. Structure of an IEEE Paper

### ✅ Mandatory Sections (in order)

1. **Title**
2. **Author(s) and Affiliation(s)**
3. **Abstract**
4. **Keywords**
5. **Introduction**
6. **Body of the Paper (Methods, Results, etc.)**
7. **Conclusion**
8. **Acknowledgment** *(if any)*
9. **References**
10. **Appendices** *(if any)*

---

## 📐 2. Page Layout and Formatting

| Attribute             | Value                     |
| --------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Paper Size            | A4 (8.27" x 11.69")       |
| Margins               | 1" (2.54 cm) on all sides |
| Font                  | Times New Roman           |
| Font Size (main text) | 10 pt                     |
| Column Format         | Two-column                |
| Spacing               | Single-spaced             |
| Paragraph Indentation | 0.25 inch (6.35 mm)       |
| Alignment             | Justified                 |

## ✍️ 3. Sections Explained

### 1. **Title**

* Centered on the first page
* Title case, boldface
* Use 24 pt Times New Roman

### 2. **Author(s) and Affiliation(s)**

* Author name(s) below the title
* Include department, university/company, city, country, and email
* Font size: 10 pt, centered

```text
John Smith, Department of ECE, ABC University, City, Country (johnsmith@abc.edu)
```

### 3. **Abstract**

* Word limit: 150–250 words
* Summarize motivation, methodology, results, and conclusion
* Font: 9 pt, justified, no citations

### 4. **Keywords**

* 3–5 keywords
* Italicize the word *Keywords* and place a colon after it.
* Example: *Keywords:* Machine Learning, Neural Networks, Image Classification

### 5. **Introduction**

* Introduce the topic, significance, background, and contributions
* Use citations as: \[1], \[2]

### 6. **Main Body**

* Divide into logical sections:

  * Methodology / Materials and Methods
  * Results and Discussion
* Use numbered headings (e.g., **2. Methodology**, **3. Results**)
* Include equations, figures, and tables

### 7. **Conclusion**

* Summarize key findings
* Mention future work or open problems

### 8. **Acknowledgment**

* Acknowledge sponsors, funding, mentors

```text
This research was supported by the IEEE Research Grant No. 123456.
```

### 9. **References**

* Use numbered citation style in square brackets
* References are listed in the order they appear in the paper
* Font size: 9 pt
* Use IEEE reference style

📌 **Example:**

```text
[1] A. Author, "Title of paper," *Journal Name*, vol. 1, no. 1, pp. 1–10, Jan. 2024.
[2] B. Author, *Book Title*, 3rd ed., City: Publisher, 2022.
[3] C. Author, "Title of paper," in *Proc. IEEE Conf. on ABC*, 2023, pp. 100–105.
```

### 10. **Appendices**

* Optional section for lengthy mathematical derivations or data tables

---

## 🔣 Equations, Figures, and Tables

### Equations:

* Center aligned
* Number equations on the right (e.g., (1))

```latex
E = mc^2 \tag{1}
```

### Figures:

* Center aligned
* Caption below the figure: "Fig. 1. Description"
* Refer in text: *as shown in Fig. 1*

### Tables:

* Caption above the table: "Table 1. Description"
* Use borders sparingly
* Refer in text: *as listed in Table 1*
